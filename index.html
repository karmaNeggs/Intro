A phone-to-phone Bluetooth mesh for when cell networks are jammed, overloaded, or not trusted: a live radar of your group, an authenticated SOS that hops device to device with distance shown in hops, and incident photos that exist on more than one phone before any single phone can be taken. No servers, no accounts, no SIM. Every handset relays traffic it cannot read, the Bluetooth advertising ID rotates each minute so passive scanning sees noise instead of a fingerprint, and GPS never touches disk. The site states plainly that it is not yet validated at real crowd scale — because it isn't, and that belongs on the front page rather than in a footnote.

RedditWatch

Who is behind India's most-seen Reddit posts? A bot and spam-influence tracker across 45 India-focused subreddits, scoring the accounts behind each subreddit's top-performing posts and comments. The score was validated against 684 accounts checked by hand against live Reddit — still active, banned, or deleted — rather than against a plausible-sounding checklist. Roughly 67 candidate signals were cut to the 10 that carried weight, a process that surfaced two real errors on the way. One finding that did not survive assumption: the accounts posting a subreddit's top content run measurably riskier than the accounts commenting on it, ~20% flagged against ~12%.

The Selection Trap

What India's competitive examination economy costs the country: foregone wage income, household coaching spend as capital misallocation, and a ten-year human-capital NPV. Religion Metamorphosis

Two studies on whether religious language is genuinely shifting toward the self — Google Books Ngram across 1800–2019, and Google Trends across 2004–2026. Hypotheses, the 20-term lexicon and the falsification rules were frozen, hashed and timestamped against four independent OpenTimestamps calendars before any data was pulled. The protocol cannot quietly move once the result arrives, and the record proving that is public.
